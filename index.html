How We Work

A calm, clear process for families

Parents should never feel lost after the first visit. Every step is explained, measured, and connected to the child's everyday life.

01

Understand the child

We begin with observation, parent discussion, and developmental concerns so the plan starts from the child's real needs.

02

Set practical goals

Goals are written for communication, behaviour, sensory needs, learning, social skills, and independence.

03

Therapy plus parent guidance

Children receive structured therapy while parents receive simple strategies they can use at home.

04

Review progress regularly

The team shares progress and adjusts the plan as the child grows, learns, and becomes more confident.
