Privacy Policy

When you visit a website related to your child, you are not just browsing information. You are looking for hope. You are searching for answers. You are trying to understand something that may feel confusing, overwhelming, or even frightening at times. At ArambH Autism Centre, Varanasi, we understand this deeply.

Humein pata hai ki jab parents online aate hain, woh sirf information nahi dhoondh rahe hote. They are looking for trust. They want to feel safe.

This Privacy Policy page is not just a legal formality. It is a simple promise - a promise that your information, your concerns, and your child's details are treated with respect and care. We want you to read this page and feel relaxed. Aap samajh sakte hain, as parents your first instinct is always to protect your child. We feel the same responsibility when you connect with us.

Why Privacy Matters So Much for Parents

For many Indian parents, talking openly about autism is still not easy. There are questions from relatives. There are misunderstandings in society. There are judgments that hurt.

Sometimes parents hesitate even to fill out a simple contact form because they fear their information might be shared. They worry about who will know. They worry about what people will think. We understand that fear.

When you share your name, your phone number, or details about your child, it is something very personal. It carries emotion. It carries trust. And we do not take that lightly. Your privacy is not just about data. It is about dignity. It is about protecting your family's peace.

What Information We May Collect

When you visit our website, we may collect some basic information. This can include:

  • Your name
  • Your contact number
  • Your email address
  • Basic details you choose to share in a message or enquiry form

We only collect what is necessary to respond to you properly. Nothing more.

If you contact us to ask about therapies, assessments, or support, we may receive information about your child that you choose to share. This may include age, concerns, or previous diagnosis details. Please know this clearly - we do not force you to share anything you are not comfortable sharing. You are always in control.

How We Use Your Information

When you fill out a form or contact us, we use your information only for simple and genuine purposes. We use it to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Call you back if you request a call
  • Provide information about therapies or guidance
  • Help you schedule appointments

That is all. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with outside marketing agencies. We do not use it to send unnecessary promotional messages. Our intention is simple - if you reach out, we respond. If you ask for help, we try to guide. Nothing more, nothing hidden.

Protecting Your Information

As parents, you know how protective you feel about your child. You lock doors. You double check school bags. You hold their hands tightly while crossing the road. In the same way, we take steps to keep your information safe.

We use secure systems and basic digital safety practices to prevent unauthorized access. Access to information is limited and handled responsibly. No system in the world is 100 percent risk free. But we make sincere efforts to protect what you share with us. Your trust matters more than anything.

Respecting Your Child's Identity

Many parents tell us that one of their biggest fears is labeling. They worry that once people know, their child may be treated differently.

On our website and in our records, we treat every child with respect. We never publicly share any child's name, image, or details without written permission from parents. If at any time we share a success story or a learning journey, it is always done with consent. And even then, we ensure privacy is maintained. Your child's story belongs to you. We honor that.

Cookies and Website Data

Like most websites, we may use basic cookies. Cookies are small files that help the website function smoothly. They may help us understand:

  • How many people visit the website
  • Which pages are most helpful
  • How users navigate the website

This information is general and not personal. It helps us improve the website experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings if you wish - it will not affect your ability to contact us. We believe in clarity. No hidden tracking. No complicated data systems.

Third Party Links

Sometimes, our website may include links to useful resources. These can be educational articles, government guidelines, or awareness materials. If you click on those links, you will move to another website.

Please remember that once you leave our website, their privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for how other websites collect or use information. We always suggest parents read privacy policies carefully before sharing personal details anywhere online. Being cautious online is always a good habit.

Your Right to Ask Questions

As a parent, you have every right to ask questions. If at any time you want to know what information we have about you, how it is being used, or if you want to update or delete your information, you can simply contact us.

There is no complicated process. No formal pressure. Just a simple conversation. We believe transparency builds trust.

Building Trust Through Understanding

ArambH Autism Centre has been serving families in Varanasi since 2019. Over the years, many parents have walked through our doors with hesitation in their eyes. They come quietly. They sit down softly. They begin speaking slowly. Sometimes, before even talking about therapy, they ask, "Yeh sab private rahega na?"

We understand why. In India, awareness is growing. But still, many families prefer to keep their journey personal. And that is completely okay. Privacy is not about hiding. It is about protecting your emotional space. We have always respected that.

Being one of the older and trusted centres in Varanasi, our relationship with families is built on confidentiality and care. Trust grows slowly. It cannot be forced. And we value every family that chooses to trust us.

A Parent's Perspective

One of the reasons privacy feels so important at ArambH is because the journey here is not only professional. It is personal. The founder, Pooja Gupta, is also a parent of an autistic child. She understands the sleepless nights. She understands the confusion after the first diagnosis. She understands the silent tears and the strong face parents show in front of relatives.

When a parent shares information, it is not seen as just a case file. It is seen as a story - a real story of a real family. And stories deserve respect. This personal understanding makes privacy not just a rule, but a responsibility felt from the heart.

Emotional Safety Is Also Privacy

Privacy is not only about data. It is also about emotional safety. When you call and speak about your child's speech delay, or repetitive behavior, or social challenges, you are sharing something sensitive. You may feel nervous. You may feel unsure.

We try to ensure that every conversation is handled gently. No judgment. No blame. No comparison. Every child grows at their own pace. Every family walks their own path. Your concerns are safe here.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Over time, small updates may happen in how we handle website data or communication systems. If there are any changes in this Privacy Policy, they will be updated on this page clearly. We believe in honesty. If something changes, you will know. There are no hidden terms.

Teaching Children About Digital Safety

As children grow, especially those who use devices for learning or therapy, digital safety becomes important. Parents often ask how to protect their children online. Here are a few simple suggestions:

  • Monitor screen time gently
  • Use parental controls if needed
  • Avoid sharing your child's diagnosis publicly on social media unless you are comfortable
  • Teach children basic safety rules in simple language

Autistic children may sometimes trust easily. It becomes even more important to guide them about online behavior as they grow older. Digital safety is a shared responsibility.

Our Commitment to Confidential Conversations

Sometimes parents visit the centre and sit quietly for a few minutes before speaking. They say, "Bas kisi ko pata nahi chalna chahiye."

We want you to know that your conversations with us remain confidential. Appointments are handled privately. Records are maintained securely. Information is not shared without your consent. Trust is fragile. Once broken, it is hard to rebuild. That is why we protect it carefully.

Supporting Families Beyond Forms

Filling out a form is just the beginning. Behind every form is a family trying to understand their child better.

Maybe you are here because your child does not respond to their name. Maybe your child avoids eye contact. Maybe teachers have raised concerns. Or maybe you just have a feeling - a mother's instinct, a father's silent worry. Whatever brought you here, your privacy will always be respected. You deserve a safe space to ask questions.

A Simple Promise

At the heart of this Privacy Policy is a simple promise:

  • Your information stays protected.
  • Your child's identity is respected.
  • Your concerns are handled with care.

If you ever have questions about privacy, safety, or how your information is handled, please reach out without hesitation. Because at the end of the day, this is not just about policies. It is about people. It is about families. It is about protecting the little hands that hold our hearts.
