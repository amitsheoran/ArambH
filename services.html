Our Services

Therapies That Make a Difference

Six specialised programs, one shared goal - helping your child grow into their best self.

Every child on the autism spectrum is unique - so no two therapy plans look the same. We start with a thorough assessment, then design a personalised program drawing from one or more of our specialised services. All under one roof, with a coordinated team that talks to each other every week.

Most Popular

ABA Therapy

Applied Behaviour Analysis - the gold standard, evidence-based intervention for autism.

ABA helps children build the skills they need most - communication, social interaction, daily living, and academic readiness - by breaking complex behaviours into small, achievable steps and reinforcing progress.

  • 1-on-1 sessions with BCBA-supervised therapists
  • Personalised goals reviewed every 4 weeks
  • Parent training included - so progress continues at home
  • Data-driven approach with monthly progress reports

Communication

Speech Therapy

Helping children find their voice - verbal, non-verbal, or somewhere in between.

Whether your child is non-verbal, has limited speech, or struggles with conversation, our speech-language pathologists work to build communication that's functional, meaningful, and tailored to them.

  • Articulation and language development
  • AAC (Augmentative & Alternative Communication) - picture boards, devices
  • Social communication and pragmatics
  • Feeding and oral-motor therapy when needed

Sensory & Motor

Occupational Therapy

Building the skills children need for everyday life - from holding a pencil to managing sensory overload.

Our OTs help children develop fine and gross motor skills, sensory processing, self-regulation, and the practical skills that make daily routines easier - for them and for you.

  • Sensory integration therapy
  • Fine motor and handwriting support
  • Self-care skills (dressing, feeding, hygiene)
  • Emotional regulation and coping strategies

Social Skills Groups

Friendship is a skill - and like any skill, it can be learned.

Small-group sessions where children practise the building blocks of social interaction: turn-taking, sharing, reading emotions, starting conversations. In a safe, low-pressure setting with peers.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How do I know which therapy my child needs?

You don't need to know! That's what the assessment is for. Our team will recommend the right combination of therapies - sometimes one, often two or three working together - based on your child's specific profile.

How long does therapy take to show results?

It varies. Some children show progress within weeks; for others, meaningful change takes months. We share monthly progress reports so you can see exactly what's happening - no guessing.

Do you work with children of all ages?

We work with children from age 18 months through 16 years. Earlier intervention generally leads to better outcomes - but it's never too late to start.

What does a typical session look like?

Most sessions are 45 minutes, one-on-one with a therapist, in a dedicated therapy room. We use play, structured activities, and visual supports - depending on your child's age and needs.

Can my child attend school and therapy together?

Yes - and many do. We schedule sessions before school, after school, or during weekends. We also offer school-shadow programs for children who need extra support during school hours.

What are your fees?

Fees vary by therapy type and session frequency. After the free consultation, we'll share a transparent fee structure with no hidden costs. Family discounts and payment plans are available.
