A Foundation of Trust

Terms & Conditions

A gentle understanding for every parent - clear, honest, and human.

Home / Terms & Conditions

When we hear the words "Terms and Conditions", many of us feel confused or even a little scared. It sounds very formal. It feels like something complicated. But at ArambH Autism Centre, Varanasi, we believe even this page should feel simple, clear, and comforting.

Aap samajh sakte hain, when you are already managing therapy sessions, school meetings, family pressure, and your child's daily needs, the last thing you want is complicated rules. That is why we want to explain everything in plain and simple words.

This page is not about legal language. It is about trust. It is about clarity. It is about making sure that every parent feels safe, respected, and understood. Let us walk through this together.

Our Shared Intention - Your Child's Growth

Everything at ArambH Autism Centre is built around one simple intention - supporting your child's development in a caring and structured way.

We understand that bringing your child to a therapy centre is not just a decision. It is an emotional step. Many parents reach here after months or even years of confusion, advice from relatives, searching on Google late at night, and quiet tears that no one else sees.

Humein pata hai that behind every admission form, there is a story. There is worry. There is hope. There is a dream that your child will grow, learn, and feel confident. Our terms are designed to protect that shared goal. They are not meant to create distance. They are meant to create understanding between families and the centre.

Admission Process - Transparency Matters

Before your child starts sessions, we conduct an assessment. This helps us understand your child's strengths, challenges, behavior patterns, communication level, and learning style.

This assessment is important because every child is different. Autism is not the same for every child. Some children speak but struggle socially. Some may not speak but understand everything. Some may have sensory sensitivities. Some may show repetitive behaviors.

We request parents to provide honest and complete information during admission. Medical history, therapy history, school reports, and behavioral concerns help us create a better plan. There is no judgment here. Only understanding.

All information shared with us is kept confidential. Your child's records are safe and not shared without permission, except when legally required.

Fees and Payment Policy - Clear and Respectful

We understand that therapy is a financial commitment. Many families adjust their budgets to ensure their child receives support.

Fees for therapy sessions are shared clearly at the time of admission. Payment timelines are also discussed in advance. We request parents to follow the payment schedule to ensure smooth continuation of services.

If there is ever a genuine difficulty, we encourage parents to communicate openly. Silence creates confusion. Conversation builds trust. Missed sessions without prior notice may not always be adjusted, because therapists reserve time specifically for your child. However, in case of medical emergencies, we try to be understanding. Everything works better when there is mutual respect.

Attendance and Consistency - Why It Matters

Therapy works best when it is consistent. Children with autism thrive on routine. They feel secure when things happen regularly. Missing too many sessions can slow progress. Sometimes, children take time to feel comfortable with therapists. When breaks are long, that comfort may reduce.

We request parents to maintain regular attendance as much as possible. If your child is unwell, please inform the centre in advance. Health and safety are important for all children attending sessions. Consistency is not pressure. It is support.

Parent Involvement - You Are Part of the Journey

At ArambH, we strongly believe therapy does not end when the session ends. Parents play the most powerful role in a child's development. A therapist may work with your child for one hour. But you are there for the rest of the day.

We may guide you with home activities, behavior strategies, communication methods, or small daily changes. These are not extra burdens. They are tools to make daily life smoother.

Sometimes parents feel tired. Sometimes they feel confused. Sometimes they feel frustrated when progress is slow. Please remember - progress in autism is not a straight line. There are good days and difficult days. Your involvement makes a difference. Even small efforts matter.

Behaviour and Safety Guidelines

We create a safe and structured environment for all children. Some children may have aggressive behaviors, self-injurious behavior, or strong emotional outbursts. These are not bad behaviors. They are communication attempts.

Our team is trained to handle such situations calmly and safely. However, we also expect parents to inform us in advance about any serious behavioral concerns. This helps us prepare properly.

Safety of every child and therapist is important. If a child's behavior becomes extremely unsafe despite interventions, we may discuss alternative strategies with parents. This is always done with care and understanding.

Use of Information and Privacy

Your child's identity, assessment reports, therapy plans, and progress details are private. We do not share photographs or videos without written consent from parents.

Sometimes we may ask permission to use anonymous case examples for awareness purposes. But this is always optional. Your trust means everything to us.

Communication Policy - Let Us Talk Openly

Clear communication prevents misunderstandings. We encourage parents to discuss concerns during scheduled meetings. Therapists may not always be available for long discussions immediately after sessions because other children are waiting.

We schedule review meetings to discuss progress, challenges, and goals. If there are concerns about therapy methods, progress, or behavior, please speak to us. Do not hold it inside. Aap akela mehsoos na karein. We are here to listen.

Therapy Goals - Realistic and Individual

One important part of our terms is understanding therapy outcomes. Every child is unique. No centre can guarantee a specific result within a fixed time. Autism is a spectrum. Progress depends on many factors - early intervention, consistency, family support, and the child's own pace.

We focus on improvement, not comparison. Sometimes progress looks small. A child making eye contact for two seconds. A child responding to their name. A child sitting for five minutes. For another parent, this may seem simple. But for you, it can feel like a miracle. Therapy is about celebrating these small wins.

Cancellation and Discontinuation

We understand that families may shift cities, change schools, or face personal circumstances. If you wish to discontinue services, we request written notice in advance as per the centre policy shared during admission. This helps us close records properly and guide you with future steps if needed.

If the centre ever needs to discontinue services due to repeated policy violations or safety concerns, this will be discussed respectfully with the family. We believe in dignity for every child and parent.

Beyond Rules and Policies

Let us pause here. Terms and Conditions are important. But behind every rule, there is a human reason.

At ArambH Autism Centre, which has been serving families in Varanasi since 2019, we have seen many journeys. Some began with denial. Some with fear. Some with anger. Some with silent acceptance. We have seen fathers who initially stayed quiet but slowly started attending sessions. We have seen mothers who blamed themselves but later found strength. We have seen grandparents who did not understand at first but later became the biggest support.

We have also seen children bloom in their own beautiful way. Bachche ki muskaan jab aati hai, it makes everything worth it.

From One Parent to Another

There is something unique about ArambH that many parents feel comforted by. The owner, Pooja Gupta, is also a parent of an autistic child. This is not just a professional journey for her. It is personal. She understands therapy rooms not only as a founder but as a mother. She knows the late-night worries. She knows the school admission struggles. She knows the comments from relatives.

That is why many policies here are built with empathy. Because humein pata hai, autism is not just about therapy sessions. It is about daily life.

Respecting Boundaries - A Two-Way Commitment

Just as parents expect care and professionalism, the centre also expects respect for therapists and staff. Therapists work with emotional intensity. They invest patience, energy, and compassion.

Kind communication, timely payments, and respectful behavior create a healthy environment. When both sides respect each other, the child benefits the most.

Online Presence and Website Use

If you are reading this on our website, please know that the information shared is for awareness and guidance. Content on the website should not be copied, misused, or reproduced without permission.

Information provided online does not replace professional assessment. Every child requires individual evaluation. If parents choose to contact us through forms or calls, the information shared will be used only to respond to queries and provide relevant support. Your privacy remains important.

Acceptance of Terms

By enrolling your child or using our services, you agree to the centre's guidelines and policies shared during admission. These policies may be updated when required to improve functioning and safety. Any changes will be communicated clearly.

This agreement is not about control. It is about clarity. Clarity prevents confusion. Confusion creates stress. And we want to reduce stress, not increase it.

Walking Together

At the end of this page, we want to say something simple. Autism parenting is not easy. Some days feel heavy. Some days feel hopeful. Some days you may question everything. But you are not alone.

ArambH Autism Centre has been walking with families in Varanasi since 2019. Over the years, we have learned that real change does not happen in big dramatic moments. It happens in small daily efforts - when a child learns to wait for their turn, when a parent stops blaming themselves, when a family starts accepting their child fully.

Terms and Conditions are just written words. But behind these words is a promise - to maintain honesty, safety, and mutual respect. If you ever feel confused about any policy, ask us. If you ever feel overwhelmed, talk to us. If you ever feel proud of your child's small achievement, share it with us. Because this journey is shared. And every step forward, no matter how small, matters.

Final Thoughts

Parenting a child with autism teaches patience in a new way. It teaches you to celebrate small things. It teaches you to listen without words. We hope this Terms and Conditions page feels different from others you may have read - not heavy, not complicated, not distant. Just clear. Just honest. Just human.

Aap samajh sakte hain, when trust is strong, everything becomes easier. We are here to support. To guide. To listen. And most importantly, to walk beside you with respect and hope.
